Steve here.

I make the internet pretty. I tell my kids I want to be a rock star when I grow up. A critic once said I obsess over specificity.

By day, a large tech company pays me to code interfaces. I advocate for digital accessibility and beauty. By night I rock with vigor and make trippy art.
