Polyrhythmic Design

  1. Lose count. Hit the downbeat.

    polytoggle, a polyrhythmic and polymeter sequencer. Loops of different lengths all land on the first beat.

  2. Pick a palette. Hear a chord.

    polycolor, a color wheel based on terahertz. Hue sets the pitch. Brightness sets the volume.

  3. Twelve columns walk into a bar.

    polygrid shows how musical rhythm and grids relate. Three columns and four meet at twelve.
